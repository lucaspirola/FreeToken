# 256K KV for K2-Horizon on the RTX 5080: design (Opus specialist, 2026-09-23; [V] verified, [I] inferred)

Owner challenge, verbatim: "I challenge you to create something that will make it fast in our rtx5080, with 256k kv 8-bits, or at least 6-bits k and 5-bits V".

## Verdict
Resident 256K KV is impossible on this box: 25.5 GiB at q8_0 and 18 GiB at K6/V5, against 16 GB of VRAM that already holds ~20.6 GB of weights worth of demand. Streaming the whole KV every step gives ~2 tok/s (27.4 GB over 56 GB/s PCIe).

The design: **exact dense prefill + query-aware sparse decode over a tiered KV.**
- The stored KV stays at q8_0 or K6/V5; the only approximation is which pages decode reads.
- Tiers:
  - Quest page bounds and a hot page cache on the GPU;
  - a small pinned-RAM warm tier;
  - the full KV at home on NVMe.
- Decode reads only VRAM or pinned RAM. A page that exists only on NVMe is skipped, promoted by a host thread, and the step is replayed. Greedy output is unaffected; a miss costs latency only.

## Budget at 256K q8_0
- **VRAM:** runtime 1.3; dense EXL3 3.1; Quest metadata 0.75 (2 KiB per 32-token page per layer, fp8 min/max); hot KV 1.8 (sink 64 + window 2,048 + 16,384-token page cache per layer); expert arena ~8.4 GiB.
- **RAM:** expert banks 15.97; process ~2.7; >= 4 GiB free (rule); warm KV tier ~2.3 GiB pinned.
- **NVMe:** 25.5 GiB (q8_0) or 18 GiB (K6/V5) per session.
- **Decode [I]:** budget of 4,096 selected tokens + 2,112 resident, 90% page-cache hit rate.
  - Attention ~5 ms/token.
  - Base EXL3 decode 12-20 ms, unmeasured: the dominant unknown.
  - => ~38-60 tok/s; ~20 tok/s if every term doubles.
- **Prefill [I]:** exact, attention-kernel-bound. Scaled from Ornith's measured 37 TFLOP/s: ~16-22 min at 256K, 4-5.5 min at 128K, 1-1.4 min at 64K. Prefixes persist on NVMe, so a document is paid for once; a follow-up turn at 256K costs ~4 s of NVMe reads.
- **Rejected:**
  - full streaming (a), 2 tok/s;
  - resident q4_0 (does not fit);
  - ~2-bit KV (below the owner's floor);
  - NVMe alone (d), 0.2 tok/s.

## Accuracy measurement
- **Sparsity, in isolation:** FreeToken's tiered mode with budget = all pages is an exact reference at 256K locally. Measure `needles.py` (7 types), `recall.py`, first-token KL, the attention mass captured, and NLL on the last 2K tokens.
- **KV quantization, in isolation:** the owner's validation run, with exllamav3's fp16/k8v8/k6v5/k4v4 arms at 8K-256K. Caveat: exllamav3's cache is H32-rotated with companding, while FreeToken's q6_0/q5_0 are unrotated, so exllamav3's K6V5 is an optimistic proxy.
- **Proposed gates:**
  - recall 3/3 at 64K/128K/256K;
  - needles: sparse >= exact on types 1 and 7, at most 1 worse on types 2-6;
  - median sparse KL <= median K6V5-vs-FP16 KL;
  - attention mass captured >= 0.95 median, >= 0.8 p5.

## Where it lives (fork-only new files)
- New files: `kvcache/tiered_pool.py`, `kvcache/page_meta.py`, `attention/tiered_sparse.py`, `kernel/triton/kv_tier.py`, `scheduler/kv_tier_io.py`.
- Hooks in `server/args.py`, the backend registry, `plan_cache_budget`, and the scheduler replay hook.
- Reuses existing pieces:
  - `qsa_block_topk` (`kernel/triton/qsa/topk.py:351`);
  - `decode_paged_attention(indices=)`, which already handles the q8/q8, q8/q6 and q6/q5 lanes (`kernel/triton/attention.py:1123-1175`);
  - the host-alias gather the expert cache already uses inside decode graphs.
- Composes with:
  - growable KV: resident below ~26K tokens, and the hot tier can be resized;
  - the radix prefix cache: logical home slots, with capacity up to the NVMe size;
  - session spill: a tiered session is already at home on NVMe;
  - single lane;
  - CUDA graphs: no host syncs; fixed grids.

## Phases

| P0, shadow mode on Ornith | The selector runs beside dense attention; output unchanged | Attention mass vs budget/page size, page-cache hit rate, NVMe-miss share. **These decide the rest.** Ornith: 10 of its 40 layers are full-attention, 256K native context, RoPE theta 1e7, dense KV fits as the reference. |
| P1, sparse on resident KV | Selection on, KV still on the GPU | Budget = all pages gives the same tokens as dense; needle gates at 64K/128K/250K on Ornith |
| P2, RAM tier + GPU page cache | | Tokens identical to P1, no host sync (nsys), decode within 10% of P1 |

## Risks
- Prefill at 256K takes 16-22 min (compute-bound).
- Top-k selection is weak on aggregation, counting and multi-hop (MagicPIG, RULER).
- RAM/OOM: the warm tier is sized once at start; O_DIRECT keeps the page cache flat.
- GPUDirect Storage is probably unavailable on WSL (hence the replay design).
- **Biggest unknown:** EXL3 base decode speed in FreeToken. If it is slower than ~30 ms/token, 20 tok/s at 256K is out of reach whatever the KV design.
